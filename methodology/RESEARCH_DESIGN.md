## 1. Final Main Research Question

> **"To what extent are changes in Arsenal Football Club’s financial resources and investment decisions associated with changes in sporting performance between 2015/16 and 2024/25?"**

### Methodological Nature of the Project
This study is fundamentally an **associational longitudinal case study**. 

The phrase *"associated with"* is deliberate, strict, and mandatory. It must **not** be replaced with causal terminology such as:
- *"caused"*
- *"influenced"*
- *"determined"*
- *"drove"*
- *"resulted in"*

Unless a valid, formally proven quasi-experimental or causal identification strategy is established later, all empirical evaluations, discussions, and conclusions must remain strictly within the boundaries of association, co-movement, and historical description.

---

## 2. Final Six Sub-Research Questions (SQ1–SQ6)

Following the Phase 1 feasibility audit, the previous preliminary list of eight sub-research questions has been consolidated. **SQ7 and SQ8 have been completely removed** as standalone questions. 

The research design is locked with **exactly six** sub-research questions:

* **SQ1 — Financial Evolution:**
  > *"How did Arsenal’s financial resources and financial structure evolve between 2015/16 and 2024/25?"*
  * *Analytical Scope:* Composition and trajectory of football turnover (matchday, broadcast, commercial), staff costs, debt profile (stadium bond redemption vs shareholder borrowing), cash reserves, and operating profitability.

* **SQ2 — Finance $\to$ Investment:**
  > *"How were changes in Arsenal’s financial resources associated with changes in transfer investment?"*
  * *Analytical Scope:* Longitudinal association between available revenue/operating cash capacity and both balance-sheet capitalized player registration additions (IAS 38) and secondary gross/net transfer market outlays.

* **SQ3 — Investment $\to$ Performance:**
  > *"To what extent was transfer investment associated with subsequent sporting performance?"*
  * *Analytical Scope:* Temporal association between capital additions / transfer market expenditures in period $t$ and subsequent sporting performance (league points, goal difference, league standing) in period $t+1$.

* **SQ4 — Wages $\to$ Performance:**
  > *"What was the relationship between wage expenditure and subsequent sporting performance?"*
  * *Analytical Scope:* Evaluating the sports economics hypothesis (Szymanski, 2014) regarding the explanatory dominance of staff costs / employee compensation relative to domestic league outcomes and points accumulation. (Note: staff_costs is not a player-only wage variable; it represents aggregate club-wide employee remuneration).

* **SQ5 — Finance $\to$ Performance:**
  > *"How were Arsenal’s overall financial resources associated with changes in sporting performance?"*
  * *Analytical Scope:* Direct co-movement between overall revenue scale, operating cash capacity, and sporting stability across the decade.

* **SQ6 — Investment Quality / Squad & Team Performance:**
  > *"Was the scale of transfer investment associated with changes in squad and team performance?"*
  * *Analytical Scope:* Assessing whether higher transfer outlays coincided with changes in team tactical efficiency and underlying sporting performance metrics (xG, xGA, xGD, PPDA, shots, goal difference). (Note: Player-level minutes and squad rejuvenation are not part of the master panel and are not measured).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Removal of Preliminary SQ7 & SQ8:**
> - Former SQ7 (*Domestic vs European Performance*) is **no longer a separate research question**; cup and European records are integrated as performance dimensions within the Sporting Performance variable block.
> - Former SQ8 (*Underlying Advanced Metrics*) is **no longer a separate research question**; underlying metrics ($xG$, $xGA$, $xGD$, $PPDA$) serve as tactical outcome measures within the Sporting Performance variable block.
> - No replacement or hidden sub-questions exist. Exactly six sub-questions are locked.

## 3. Final Research Period & Sample Boundary

* **Temporal Window:** Exactly 10 completed football seasons: **2015/16 through 2024/25**.
* **Sample Boundary Constraint:** The 2025/26 season is **strictly excluded** from the core longitudinal sample. Ongoing or uncompleted seasons introduce partial-year accounting distortions, survivorship bias, and incomplete reporting periods.
* **Sample Size:** $N = 10$ annual observations.

---

## 4. Units of Analysis

1. **Primary Unit of Analysis:** 
   $$\text{Arsenal FC} \times \text{Football Season} \quad (N = 10)$$
   Macro-level longitudinal panel linking audited annual financial reporting periods (years ended 31 May) to completed sporting campaigns.
2. **Secondary Units of Analysis:**
   - **Transfer Transaction:** Individual player registration acquisitions or disposals ($N \approx 60\text{--}80$ major transfers).
   - **Player-Season:** Individual squad member minutes, appearances, and contributions.
   - **Match:** Match-by-match league fixtures ($N = 380$ Premier League games).
   - **Financial Reporting Period:** Annual accounting years ended 31 May (FY2016 through FY2025).

## 6. Integration of Advanced Performance Metrics

Advanced tactical metrics do **not** constitute a standalone research question. Instead, they function as intermediate and underlying outcome measures within the **Sporting Performance** component:

* **Eligible Metrics (Phase 1 Feasibility Compliant):**
  - Expected Goals ($xG$)
  - Expected Goals Against ($xGA$)
  - Expected Goal Difference ($xGD = xG - xGA$)
  - Expected Points ($xPTS$)
  - Passes Per Defensive Action ($PPDA$)
  - Possession %
  - Shots & Shots on Target Attempted / Conceded
* **Exclusion Protocol (Rule 3):** Event-level tracking metrics (pressures, pressure regains, progressive passes, progressive carries, shot-creating actions) that lack historical coverage for 2015/16 and 2016/17 are **strictly excluded** from the core longitudinal panel.

## 7. Integration of Domestic & European Competitions

Domestic cups and European competitions do **not** constitute a standalone research question. They are multi-dimensional sporting output indicators evaluated alongside league performance:

* **Premier League (Continuous & Ordinal):** Final Position, Points, PPG, Wins, Draws, Losses, Goals For, Goals Against, Goal Difference.
* **Domestic Cups (Categorical):** FA Cup Stage Reached, FA Cup Trophy, League Cup Stage Reached, League Cup Trophy.
* **European Competitions (Categorical & Continuous):** UEFA Competition Tier (UCL, UEL, None), Stage Reached, Knockout Progression, Official UEFA Club Coefficient Points.
* **Prohibition on Synthetic Indexing:** Domestic, European, and league results must **not** be blended into an arbitrary, unvalidated composite "overall success score." Each competitive track must remain analytically distinct.

## 8. Final Empirical Logic

The quantitative investigation centers on four primary relationships:

```
A. FINANCIAL RESOURCES ─────────► INVESTMENT
   (e.g., Football Turnover_t ──► Player Additions_{t+1})

B. INVESTMENT ──────────────────► SPORTING PERFORMANCE
   (e.g., Player Additions_t ───► Points_{t+1}, xGD_{t+1})

C. WAGES ───────────────────────► SPORTING PERFORMANCE
   (e.g., Staff Costs_t ────────► Points_{t+1}, xGD_{t+1})

D. FINANCIAL RESOURCES ─────────► SPORTING PERFORMANCE
   (e.g., Football Turnover_t ──► Points_{t+1})
```

All four pathways represent proposed **associations and co-movements**, not proven causal effects.

## 9. Lag Structure & Sample Size Mechanics

To reflect the temporal reality of football management—where financial windfalls fund subsequent transfer windows, and newly acquired players require time to integrate:

* **Primary Specification:** One-season lag:
  $$\text{Variable}_t \longrightarrow \text{Outcome}_{t+1}$$
  *Examples:*
  - $\text{Football Turnover}_t \longrightarrow \text{Player Additions}_{t+1}$
  - $\text{Player Additions}_t \longrightarrow \text{Points}_{t+1}$
  - $\text{Staff Costs}_t \longrightarrow \text{Points}_{t+1}$
* **Sample Size Impact:** Introducing a 1-year lag reduces the effective sample from $N = 10$ to **$N = 9$**.
* **Two-Year Lags ($t+2$):** A 2-year lag reduces the sample to **$N = 8$** and should only be applied where there is a compelling theoretical rationale (e.g., multi-year contract maturation). Lags must never be applied mechanically.

---

## 10. Statistical & Methodological Restrictions ($N = 10$)

Because the research window consists of exactly 10 completed seasons ($N = 10$, reducing to $N \approx 9$ with lags):

1. **Prohibition of Primary Multivariable OLS:** Conventional multivariable regression with multiple covariates ($k \ge 3$) leaves $df \le 5$, resulting in extreme overfitting, severely inflated standard errors, and statistically meaningless $p$-values. Multivariable OLS is prohibited as the primary empirical strategy.
2. **Permissible Statistical Toolkit:**
   - Descriptive longitudinal trajectory analysis (indexed baselines, growth rates).
   - Bivariate Spearman rank-order correlation ($\rho$) and Kendall’s rank correlation ($\tau$).
   - Bivariate Pearson correlation ($r$) strictly where linearity and distributional properties are defensible.
   - Lagged association analysis ($t \to t+1$).
   - Standardized metric comparisons ($z$-scores).
   - Sensitivity analysis testing outlier influence and the COVID-19 disruption period.
3. **Multicollinearity Awareness:** Wage expenditure, total revenue, and amortisation exhibit high secular co-trending ($r > 0.85$), making coefficient isolation impossible in an $N=10$ sample.
4. **No Spurious Significance Claims:** No claims of statistical significance may be asserted without explicit reference to small-sample power limitations and exact permutation benchmarks.

## 12. Core Zero-Trust Research Principles (Rules 1–12)

1. **RULE 1 (Verification):** NO VERIFIABLE SOURCE = NO OBSERVATION.
2. **RULE 2 (Defensibility):** NO PRIMARY OR DEFENSIBLE SECONDARY SOURCE = DO NOT USE THE VARIABLE.
3. **RULE 3 (Coverage):** NO COMPARABLE HISTORICAL COVERAGE = EXCLUDE THE VARIABLE FROM LONGITUDINAL ANALYSIS.
4. **RULE 4 (No Fabrication):** NEVER INVENT, IMPUTE, ESTIMATE, SIMULATE, OR FABRICATE historical observations.
5. **RULE 5 (No Arbitrary Formulas):** NEVER create values using arbitrary formulas/constants to replace missing observations.
6. **RULE 6 (Provenance):** Every observation must have verifiable provenance detailing source, filing date, page, and table.
7. **RULE 7 (Conceptual Separation):** Transfer-market fee data must remain conceptually separate from accounting player-acquisition costs and amortisation.
8. **RULE 8 (Calendar Alignment):** Financial reporting periods and football seasons must NOT be assumed identical; alignment mechanics must be explicitly documented.
9. **RULE 9 (Prerequisite Audit):** Do not perform econometrics until the source and comparability audit has passed.
10. **RULE 10 (Causal Restraint):** Do not claim causality unless a valid identification strategy is formally established.
11. **RULE 11 (Historical Integrity):** Do not use current information to retrospectively reconstruct historical observations unless independently verifiable.
12. **RULE 12 (Conservative Missingness):** If a source cannot be accessed or verified, mark it as unavailable rather than guessing.

## 13. Preserved Phase 1 Feasibility Findings

All empirical findings from Phase 1 remain preserved and foundational:
1. **Financial Data Feasibility:** 100% auditable via Companies House filings of Arsenal Holdings Limited (Company No. 04250459), audited by Deloitte LLP.
2. **COVID-19 Financial Distortion:** The 2019/20 season extension deferred £34m in broadcast revenue to FY2021; FY2021 matchday revenue collapsed to £3.8m due to behind-closed-doors fixtures.
3. **Transfer Bifurcation:** Official club statements designate almost all fees as "undisclosed." Market fees require secondary sources (Transfermarkt) and must never be conflated with balance-sheet additions (IAS 38).
4. **Advanced Metrics Break:** Granular event metrics (StatsBomb pressures, progressive carries) only begin in 2017/18 and are excluded under Rule 3. Understat metrics ($xG$, $xGA$, $PPDA$) are consistent across all 10 seasons.
5. **Sample Constraints:** $N = 10$ ($N = 9$ with 1-year lag) restricts analysis to non-parametric correlation and structural trajectory tracking.
